How it works

Three steps.
One beautiful card.

No setup. No account. Just paste and go.

01
Paste
Click the Unfurl icon in your Chrome toolbar. Drop any link into the box. Your blog post, that GitHub repo, the Notion doc you keep sharing.
02
Unfurl
AI reads the page, writes a beautiful one-line description, and picks the perfect color palette. The card appears in seconds.
03
Share
Download as PNG, copy to clipboard, or drop straight into Notion. Your link is now something worth looking at.
